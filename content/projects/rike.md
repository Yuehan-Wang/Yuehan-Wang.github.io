日课 ("daily lesson") is how I keep learning outside work: AI agents, agentic security, and data platforms on Azure.

I pick a topic, Claude writes a lesson based on what I already know, and then quizzes me. I need 90% before anything that builds on it unlocks, so I can't fake my way through. It skips what AI can just write for me, like query syntax, and spends the time on how things actually work and how to tell when an AI's answer is wrong.

A short industry brief and podcast also land in my inbox every morning.
