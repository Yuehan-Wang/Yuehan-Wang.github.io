日课 ("daily lesson") is how I keep learning outside work.

It covers three tracks: agentic workflows, agentic security, and data platforms on Azure (Delta Lake, storage networking, identity). When I open a topic, Claude writes the lesson from my learner profile. A 5-question quiz follows, graded on the spot. A score of 90% or more marks the topic as mastered, and only then do the topics that depend on it unlock. The profile is updated after every quiz, so the next lesson starts from what I actually got wrong. It works in Chinese or English.

The course skips what AI can already write for me, like query syntax and CLI flags. It spends its time on mental models, trade-offs, failure modes, and how to tell whether an AI's answer is right.

Every morning a separate job on my VM emails me a short industry brief with a two-voice podcast.
